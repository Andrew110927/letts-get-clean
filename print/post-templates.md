# Posts for Nextdoor and local Facebook groups

Three short posts to copy and paste. Everything in them is true and on the website today. No made-up reviews and no "only 2 spots left!" pressure.

**The link.** These use the Nextdoor link (`?src=nextdoor`), so a booking from it shows "Nextdoor" under "How did you hear about us?". Posting in a Facebook group? Change `nextdoor` at the end of the link to `facebook`.

**Before you post:**
- Read each group's rules. Many Facebook groups only allow business posts on a certain day, and some Nextdoor neighborhoods want them in a business or "for sale" section.
- Add a photo or video. The before-and-after photos and videos on the website already have the license plates blurred; use those. For any new photo, blur the plate first. The mascot picture works too.
- Post one at a time, a week or so apart, rather than all three on one day.
- Answer comments yourself. A real reply from Andrew does more than anything in the post.
