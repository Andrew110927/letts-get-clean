Refund and cancellation policy

Effective and last updated October 31, 2026

The short version

  • There's no deposit, so there's nothing to refund upfront. You pay after the job.
  • Cancel or reschedule any time, for free.
  • Rain or freezing weather means a new time, free.
  • Not happy with something? Tell Andrew before you pay, and he'll fix it then.

No deposit, pay after

You don't pay anything when you book. You pay after the job is done, with cash, Cash App or Venmo, so you see the work before any money changes hands.

Cancelling or rescheduling

Cancelling or rescheduling is free, at any time. Call or text Andrew at (845) 282-9387. The sooner you tell him, the better, so someone else can take the spot. If you can't make it and forget to tell us, there's still no fee, but please text when you can.

Weather and other delays

If rain or freezing temperatures are in the forecast for an outside job, Andrew will text you to pick a new time, at no cost. If he's running late or has to cancel for any reason, he'll text you, and you can pick a new time or cancel for free.

If you're not happy

Before you pay, look the work over with Andrew. If anything was missed, he'll take care of it right then. If you notice a problem after he leaves, call or text him with a photo and he'll talk it through with you.

When we give refunds

Because you pay after you've seen the work, payments are normally final. We refund money when:

  • you were charged for a service or extra that wasn't done,
  • you were charged the wrong amount or charged twice, or
  • there's a problem we agree we caused and can't fix.

Depending on what happened, the refund covers the whole job or the part that was affected, and Andrew will tell you which and why.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back the way you paid: cash in cash, Cash App or Venmo back to the same account, and card payments back to the same card. Andrew answers refund requests within 2 days and sends agreed refunds within 7 days. Card refunds can take 5 to 10 business days to show on your statement, depending on your bank.

If you pay by card online

  • Your card is held for the total, not charged. It's only charged after the job is done, and never for more than the hold.
  • For appointments more than 5 days away, your card is saved and the hold is placed 2 days before.
  • If you or Andrew cancel, the hold is released. Your bank may take a few days to remove it from your statement.

How to ask

Call or text Andrew at (845) 282-9387, or email andrew.letts@icloud.com, with your name, the date of the job and what happened.
