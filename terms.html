Our services

We offer mobile car detailing (exterior, interior and full details), bike and e-bike details, trash can cleaning, and full house pressure washing in Orange County, New York. What each package includes and what it costs is listed on the home page.

We take care with every job, but results depend on the condition of what we clean. Some stains, odors, scratches, oxidation or damage may not come out completely. If Andrew sees that something can't be fixed, he'll tell you.

Booking

  • Book online, by text or by phone. Your time is held when you book, and Andrew texts you to confirm.
  • Please give accurate details: your address, what we're cleaning, and the vehicle size. They decide the price and what we bring.
  • A house pressure wash takes the whole day. Two cars on one visit take two back-to-back time slots.
  • Customers of any age are welcome. If you're under 13, a parent or guardian needs to book for you.

Prices and payment

  • The total in your quote when you book is the price you pay, even if our prices change before your appointment.
  • If the job turns out different from what was booked (for example a bigger vehicle than you picked), Andrew tells you the new price before starting, and you can say no. Extras such as "extra-dirty interior" are only added if you pick them or agree to them on the day.
  • One discount per booking: the one that saves you the most. Military, veteran and first responder discounts need an ID at the appointment.
  • You pay after the job with cash, Cash App or Venmo. If you choose to pay by card online, your card is held, not charged, until the job is done.

Cancelling, rescheduling and weather

You can cancel or reschedule for free at any time by calling or texting (845) 282-9387. If rain or freezing temperatures are forecast for an outside job, Andrew will text you to pick a new time. If he ever has to cancel, he'll help you find a new time. The details are in the refund and cancellation policy.

What we need from you

  • For at-home jobs, an outdoor electrical outlet and a hose spigot within reach, and permission to work at the address.
  • A safe place to park and work, with pets kept inside.
  • For interior details, take out valuables and personal items first. We're not responsible for things left in the vehicle.
  • Tell Andrew before he starts about anything he should know: loose trim, chipped paint, cracked glass, aftermarket parts, or for a house wash, problem spots like peeling paint, loose siding or open windows.
  • For drop-offs, pick up your vehicle at the time you agree on.

If we arrive and can't do the job (no water or power, no way in, or it isn't safe), we'll reschedule. There's no fee for that.

Condition check and existing damage

Before starting, Andrew may walk around the vehicle or house with you and take photos of any damage that's already there. We aren't responsible for damage that existed before the job, normal wear, or failing paint, trim or seals that a gentle wash exposes. Pressure washing can loosen paint, caulk or siding that's already coming loose, so please point those spots out first.

If you think something was damaged during the job, call or text Andrew right away with photos. He'll look at it with you and work out a fair fix.
