Accessibility statement

Effective October 31, 2026. Last updated November 1, 2026

The short version

  • We want everyone to be able to see our prices and book a detail, whatever device or assistive technology they use.
  • If anything on the site gets in your way, call or text (845) 282-9387 and Andrew will help you book and fix the problem.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Headings, labels and buttons are real HTML, so screen readers can find their way around.
  • Photos have text descriptions, and decorative pictures are skipped.
  • Text and form fields are checked for color contrast, and errors are explained in words, not just color.
  • The before and after sliders work with the arrow keys.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the page stops animating and the background video stays still.
  • The pages work when zoomed to 200% and on narrow phone screens.

Videos

  • The Hyundai Sonata video plays music with no talking, and its caption track says so. The other videos are silent. Each video has a written description, and the Sonata's results are also shown as photos on the page.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, call or text Andrew at (845) 282-9387, or email andrew.letts@icloud.com, and tell him which page and what happened. He'll help you book another way right away, and get the problem fixed as soon as he can.

Last checked

This statement was written on October 31, 2026 after automated checks (axe-core) and a keyboard-only test of every page.
