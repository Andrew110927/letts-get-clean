Privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026. Last updated November 1, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what you type when you book or leave a review, and use it to do your detail.
  • We never sell your information, share it for ads, or use tracking cookies or analytics.
  • Call or text (845) 282-9387, or email andrew.letts@icloud.com, to see, fix or delete what we have about you.

Who we are

Letts Get Clean is a mobile detailing and pressure washing business owned and run by Andrew Letts in Newburgh, New York ("we" and "us"). This policy covers this website, bookings made through it, and texts and calls with us. For any privacy question, call or text Andrew at (845) 282-9387 or email andrew.letts@icloud.com.

What we collect, and how

  • When you book online: your name, phone number, your email if you choose to give it, street address and town (not needed for drop-offs), what we're cleaning (car make and model, bikes, trash cans or your house), the day and time, your notes, how you heard about us if you tell us, whether you have an outdoor outlet and hose, and whether you're using the military and first responder discount.
  • When you leave a review: your star rating, your first name if you give it, and what you write.
  • When you text, call or email: your number or email address and what you send us.
  • If you pay by card online: you type your card on Stripe's secure payment page. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us whether the card went through and the amount held or charged.
  • On your device: if you allow "Preferences" and tick "Remember my details", your booking details are saved in your own browser so the form fills in next time. If you allow Preferences and came from a QR code on a flyer, card or sign, the browser remembers which one until you close the tab. Your cookie choice itself is kept for 12 months. None of this is sent to us on its own. See the cookie policy.
  • Technical details: like any website, our host (GitHub Pages) and our database service (Supabase) receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads prices and open times. We don't use it to identify you.

How we use it

  • To confirm your booking by text and show up at the right place with the right gear.
  • To do the job, write invoices and receipts, and keep payment records.
  • To apply discounts you qualify for.
  • To learn which flyers, cards and posts bring people in, from "How did you hear about us?".
  • To show reviews on this site, with your first name if you gave one.
  • To keep the business and tax records the law requires.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We use a few services that run the business for us, and they only get what they need to do that:

  • Supabase stores bookings, reviews and the business records behind them.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Stripe handles card payments, only if you choose to pay by card online.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.

Texts

Andrew texts the number you give to confirm and coordinate your appointment. We don't send marketing texts unless you ask for them. Message and data rates may apply. Text STOP or just tell Andrew, and he'll stop.

How long we keep it

  • Bookings, invoices and payments: for as long as we need them to serve you, and then for up to 7 years because tax rules can require it. After that we delete them.
  • Reviews: until you ask us to take yours down.
  • Texts: on Andrew's phone for as long as he needs them to handle your jobs.
  • Saved on your device: until you clear it (see cookie settings), or until you close the tab for the QR code tag.

How we protect it

The site only loads over a secure connection (HTTPS). Booking records sit in a database that the public can't read; only the owner can sign in to see them. We never store card numbers. No system is perfect, though. If your information is ever exposed in a data breach, we'll tell you without unreasonable delay, as New York's SHIELD Act requires.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can ask to see the information we have about you, correct it, or have it deleted, including your review. You can also ask us to stop texting you at any time.

  • How to ask: text or call Andrew at (845) 282-9387, or email andrew.letts@icloud.com. The button below starts a text for you.
  • How we check it's you: we reply to the phone number your booking used, so nobody else can see or delete your details. If you email us, we'll text that number to confirm it's you. If you've changed numbers, we may ask a question only you would know, such as your appointment date.
  • What we delete: your name, phone number, address, car details and notes from our booking records and customer list, and your review if you want.
  • What we have to keep: the date, the service and the amount paid for each job, because tax rules require it. Those records keep no more about you than the law needs.
  • How fast: within 30 days of your request.

We don't sell or share personal information for advertising, so there's nothing to opt out of. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, it's honored by default for the same reason.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers. It asks before using your browser's storage for anything optional, and you can change your choice any time. The details are in the cookie policy.

Children

Customers of any age are welcome. If you're under 13, a parent or guardian needs to book for you, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 sent us information, we'll delete it. For anyone under 18, we only collect what we need to do the job, and we never sell it or use it for ads.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use information you already gave us, we'll ask you first.
