Cookie policy

Effective and last updated October 31, 2026

The short version

  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or trackers.
  • It can save your booking details in your own browser, but only if you tick "Remember my details".
  • You can clear that any time in cookie settings below.

What this site stores

Instead of cookies, the site uses your browser's own storage for two small things. Neither is sent to anyone on its own.

NameProviderPurposeCategoryKept for
lgc.contact.v1This site (local storage)Fills in your name, phone, address, town and car next time you book, only if you tick "Remember my details"PreferencesUntil you clear it
lgc.srcThis site (session storage)Remembers which QR code you scanned (flyer, card, magnet or sign) to fill in "How did you hear about us?"PreferencesUntil you close the tab

The site also talks to our database service, Supabase, to load prices, open times and reviews and to save bookings. Those requests set no cookies and store nothing in your browser. The owner's sign-in page (the "Owner login" link) keeps Andrew signed in on his own devices; customers never use it.

Why there's no cookie banner

A cookie banner asks for permission before a site uses optional tracking. This site doesn't use any, and the one optional item above is only saved when you tick the box yourself. If we ever add analytics, embedded maps or videos, or anything else that sets cookies, we'll ask first with "Accept" and "Reject" buttons of equal size, and nothing will load until you choose. Global Privacy Control signals are honored by default.
