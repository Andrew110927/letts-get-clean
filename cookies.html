Cookie policy

Effective and last updated October 31, 2026

The short version

  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or trackers.
  • It only remembers your booking details, or the flyer you scanned, if you allow "Preferences".
  • Change your choice or clear what's saved any time in cookie settings below.

What this site stores

Instead of cookies, the site uses your browser's own storage for three small things. None of them is sent to anyone on its own.

The site also talks to our database service, Supabase, to load prices, open times and reviews and to save bookings. Those requests set no cookies and store nothing in your browser. The owner's sign-in page (the "Owner login" link) keeps Andrew signed in on his own devices; customers never use it.

Your choice

The first time you visit, a small panel asks whether this browser may keep Preferences. "Accept all" and "Reject all" are the same size, and "Customize" lets you choose each category. Until you choose, nothing optional is stored, so ignoring the panel is the same as saying no. Your choice is kept for 12 months, and you can change it any time below or with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as "Reject all" and don't ask. Analytics and marketing aren't used on this site at all. If we ever add them, or embedded maps or videos that set cookies, they'll stay off until you allow them here.
