# Letts Get Clean: owner's guide

This guide is for running the business day to day. It has no code in it.

You have two things:

- **The website** is what customers see. They book there.
- **LGC Pro** is your app. You use it to confirm jobs, send invoices, record payments, change prices and keep backups.

Both share the same data, so anything you change in the app shows up on the website.

## 1. Put the app on your phone (one time)

**iPhone**
1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open **LGC Pro** from your home screen and sign in with your email and password.

**Android**
1. Open the app link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮**, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

**Windows PC**
Open the app link in Chrome or Edge and sign in. To make it a desktop app, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. A bookmark works too.

Only your account can see customers and money. Anyone else who signs up sees nothing.

**Forgot your password?** On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**, then **Send reset link**, and follow the email.

### Booking alerts
Go to **More → Booking alerts** and tap **Allow**. Your phone buzzes when a customer books on the website, even with the app closed.
On iPhone this only works in the app opened from your home screen (iOS 16.4 or newer).

### Updates
Updates arrive by themselves: whenever you open the app with signal, you get the newest version. If something looks out of date, close the app all the way and open it again. The version number is at the bottom of the **More** tab.

## 2. A job from start to finish

1. **A booking comes in.** Your phone buzzes, and the job shows on **Jobs** and **Today** as **Needs OK**.
2. **Confirm it.** Tap the job, tap **Confirm**, then text the customer from the button that appears.
3. **On the day,** **Today** shows your next job with buttons to navigate, call and text.
4. **When you finish,** tap **Done** on Today's job card (or **Mark done** on the job's page). The invoice opens already filled in at the price they booked. Add anything extra (another line, a tip).
5. **Paid now?** On that same screen, tap how they paid: Cash, Cash App or Venmo. If they're paying later, leave it on **Not yet**. Then tap **Save invoice**.
6. **Send it.** **Share the invoice PDF** sends a branded invoice by Messages, Mail or any app. **Text** sends a message with tap-to-pay Cash App and Venmo links, amount already filled in. Sending a draft marks it as sent.
7. **Paid later?** Open the invoice and tap the method under **Payments**. For half now and half later, tap **Part payment, other amount or date**.

## 3. Invoices and quotes (Invoices tab)

- **New invoice:** tap **+**. Pick the customer, car and services. You can change any line's price, add your own line, take money off ($ or %) or add a tip.
- **Quotes:** tap **New quote**. If the customer says yes, open the quote after the job and tap **Turn into invoice**.
- **What the labels mean:**
  - **Draft:** not sent yet.
  - **Sent:** waiting for payment.
  - **Partially Paid** and **Paid:** worked out from the payments you record.
  - **Overdue:** past its due date with money still owed.
  - **Void:** cancelled.
- **Made a mistake?** Invoices are never deleted, so your records stay complete. Instead:
  - **Void** cancels an invoice. It stays in your records, crossed out. If it has payments, remove those first.
  - **Duplicate** starts a corrected copy.
  - **Remove** next to a payment takes it off. It stays listed, crossed out.

## 4. Customers (Clients tab)

Every booking is filed under its customer automatically by phone number, along with their car. You can add notes like gate codes or where the hose is. **Hide this customer** takes someone off your list without deleting their history.

## 5. Money (More → Money)

- The four tiles show money in for **this week, this month, this year and all time**. Tap one to see its details: profit, tips, expenses, jobs done and your average job.
- **Who owes you** lists unpaid invoices with the late ones first. Tap one to open it.
- **Expenses:** tap **+** to log gas, supplies, equipment or marketing. Profit takes them off.
- Money counts **on the day you were paid**, and jobs count **on the day of the job**. So if someone pays this week for last week's job, the week can show money with 0 jobs. That's correct.

## 6. Changing prices and settings

### More → Settings (changes the live website as soon as you save)

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Contact | The phone number customers call or text. |
| Launch deal | The last day of launch prices. The day after, the website switches to regular prices by itself. Clear it to end the deal now. |
| Discounts & promos | Military and first responders, the two-car deal, and holiday promos. A booking gets one discount, whichever saves the customer most. |
| Prices | The deal price and the regular price for each package. |
| Vehicle sizes | The extra charge for bigger vehicles. |
| Extras & add-on services | Add-ons and their prices. New ones are for invoices only until you switch them on for the website. |
| Bikes & house washing | One price each. A house wash books the whole day. |
| Open times | Your first day, start times on weekdays and weekends, and days off. |
| Social links | Shown on the website and the Social screen. |

Tap **Save · updates the website** when you're done, then open the website to check.

### More → Invoice settings (only on your invoices, not the website)

- **Business details:** the name, phone, email and website printed on invoices. Your home address is never printed.
- **Payment options:** your Cash App and Venmo. They show on every invoice with tap-to-pay links and QR codes.
- **Numbers, tax and due date:**
  - The invoice and quote prefixes (LGC-, Q-).
  - **Sales tax %**: keep it at 0% until your accountant confirms the New York rules.
  - How many days until an invoice is overdue.
- **Wording:** the thank-you note and terms at the bottom of each invoice.
- **Logo and color:** the logo and brand color on the PDF.

## 7. Reports for your accountant (More → Reports & backup)

- **Spreadsheets:** pick the dates (or tap **This month**, **Last year** and so on), then tap:
  - **Invoices** for every invoice issued in those dates.
  - **Payments** for every payment that came in. The total matches the Money screen.

  The files open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.
- **Year summary:** pick the year and tap **Download the summary**. It shows money in month by month, split into services, tips and sales tax, plus expenses and profit. **This is the file to send your accountant.**

## 8. Backups (please do this monthly)

Your database plan doesn't keep backups you can download, so this button is your safety net.

1. Go to **More → Reports & backup** and tap **Download backup**.
2. Check the counts it shows (customers, bookings, invoices…), then tap **Save the file**.
3. **iPhone:** choose **Save to Files**, or Mail it to yourself. **PC:** it goes to your Downloads folder.

- **How often:** once a month, and before any big change.
- **Where:** somewhere other than your phone, like your email or Google Drive.
- **Keep it private:** it has customers' names and phone numbers.
- **Getting data back from a backup** is a job for a developer (or Claude). The app can't load a backup by itself, on purpose: one wrong tap could overwrite your real records.

## 10. When something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No connection. Try again when you have signal." | The app still opens and shows your last data, but saving needs signal. Try again when you have bars. |
| "Your sign-in expired." | Sign in again. |
| The app looks out of date | Close it all the way and open it again with signal. |
| Booking alerts stopped | **More → Booking alerts**: turn it off, then on again. |
| A red message when saving | It says what's wrong, for example "That's more than the balance due." Fix that and save again. |
| "…needs a one-time database update" | Ask a developer (or Claude) to apply the latest Letts database update. Everything else keeps working. |

## 11. Ask a developer (or Claude) for these

- Letting another person sign in to the app.
- Getting data back from a backup.
- Database updates.
- Card payments (in progress on the website side; it needs your Stripe account).

## 12. Good habits

- **Your home address never goes on the website or invoices.** Drop-off customers get it by text.
- **Blur license plates** in every photo and video before you post it.
- **Never paste passwords or keys** into texts, emails or chats.
- **Back up once a month.**
